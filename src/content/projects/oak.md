Oak was an iOS fitness app for experienced lifters. It integrated workout logging, program management, check-ins, and Apple Health data with an AI coach grounded in each user's training history.

## Approach

Oak was built for experienced lifters on purpose. They already tracked everything. What they lacked was one place where logs, recovery data, and check-ins informed the same coaching decisions.

- Strength and cardio training programs
- Workout logging with long-term performance history
- Check-ins covering training, recovery, and subjective feedback
- Apple Health integration through HealthKit
- Conversational coaching and scheduled reports built on stored training data

## Stack

- React Native, Expo, and Expo Router
- Convex for data, authentication, and application state
- HealthKit, RevenueCat, Sign in with Apple, and push notifications
- Next.js site for landing, support, and privacy pages
