Nyx was a conversational iOS sleep coach. Users described what was affecting their sleep in chat, and the assistant combined that context with onboarding and profile data to recommend specific habit changes.

## Approach

Chat served as the primary interface because sleep quality depends on factors a single score misses, such as stress, schedule, and environment. The app also included experimental voice and audio interactions alongside text.

## Stack

- React Native, Expo, and Expo Router
- Vercel AI SDK with OpenAI
- Sign in with Apple, secure storage, and RevenueCat
- Next.js site for landing, support, and privacy pages
